# Submission Scope and Reproduction Limits

This package copy does not include the authoritative frozen-experiment list or a complete reproducibility manifest. The exact mapping from every manuscript claim to its frozen input, analysis script, and output must be completed before the package is described as a full replication package.

Directories outside the manuscript's evidence chain may contain exploratory work or material from the broader DyMAM project. Their presence does not make them part of the paper's reported evidence.

The included scripts support selected checks using saved result files. They do not rerun the original reconnaissance collection or the complete pipeline from raw inputs.
